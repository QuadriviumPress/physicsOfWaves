# The Physics of Waves

by [Howard Georgi](https://phys.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Waves_and_Acoustics/The_Physics_of_Waves_(Goergi)) (Harvard University).

This MyST edition is a web-native conversion of the structured
[LibreTexts](https://phys.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Waves_and_Acoustics/The_Physics_of_Waves_(Goergi)) edition of Georgi's open textbook
on vibrations and waves.
